# DE Terminology — Daily Practice Prompt

We are continuing my Data Engineering Terminology learning.

My goal is to become fluent in Data Engineering terminology for interviews — not to memorize textbook definitions.

I am maintaining my daily terminology files here:

11_Notes/DE_Terminology/

Example:
Day_01_DE_Terminology.md
Day_02_DE_Terminology.md
...

## Daily Learning Process

Follow this sequence strictly.

### STEP 1 — Previous Keyword Recall

Before introducing any new keywords, test me on previously learned terminology.

- Ask 5–10 short recall questions.
- Mix keywords from previous days.
- Do NOT always test the most recent keywords.
- Focus on relationships and distinctions between concepts.
- Do not give me the answers initially.
- Let me answer in my own words.

Examples:

- What does Watermark immediately make you think of?
- What is the difference between ETL and ELT?
- How does CDC relate to Incremental Load?
- What is the purpose of Data Ingestion?
- What is the difference between Full Load and Incremental Load?

After I answer:

1. Evaluate my understanding.
2. Mark it as Correct / Partially Correct / Incorrect.
3. Give a concise correction where necessary.
4. Highlight important terminology I missed.
5. Reinforce the connection between related concepts.

Do NOT over-explain.

---

### STEP 2 — Introduce Today's 10 Keywords

After completing the recall exercise, introduce exactly 10 new Data Engineering keywords.

Do NOT give me their definitions initially.

The 10 keywords must:

- Be logically connected.
- Build on concepts I already know where appropriate.
- Come from different areas of Data Engineering over time.
- Support mid-level/senior Data Engineer interview preparation.
- Avoid random keyword selection.

Do NOT introduce Kafka or streaming technologies yet.

---

### STEP 3 — My Attempt

Ask me to explain all 10 keywords in my own words.

I should attempt:

1. What is it?
2. Why is it used?
3. What does it connect to?

Do not provide the answers until I attempt them.

---

### STEP 4 — Correct My Understanding

After my attempt:

For each keyword:

- Identify what I got right.
- Correct inaccuracies.
- Add only the important missing terminology.
- Keep explanations concise.
- Point out relationships with other keywords.

Do not turn the discussion into textbook notes.

---

### STEP 5 — Final Sticky Version

After corrections, produce the final terminology entries using exactly:

### Keyword

**Meaning:** One concise explanation.

**Think:** 2–4 words/phrases that trigger the concept.

**Connect:** Related concepts/keywords.

Example:

### Fact Table

**Meaning:** Stores business events and measurable metrics.

**Think:** What happened? How much?

**Connect:** Grain → Dimension → Measures

---

### STEP 6 — Key Connections

After the 10 entries, identify the important relationships between today's concepts.

Use a structure such as:

## Key Connections

### Concept Flow

A → B → C → D

### Relationship

A → Purpose

B → Mechanism

C → Outcome

Focus on mental connections rather than detailed explanations.
