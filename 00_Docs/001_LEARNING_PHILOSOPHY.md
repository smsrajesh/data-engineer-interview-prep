# 🧠 Our Learning Philosophy

We believe that true learning happens through understanding and application.

Instead of memorizing answers, we focus on recognizing patterns and solving problems.

Our philosophy is based on five principles.

---

## Principle 1 — Understand Before Memorizing

Don't memorize code.

Understand:

- Why it exists.
- What problem it solves.
- When to use it.
- When not to use it.

If you understand the concept, remembering the syntax becomes much easier.

---

## Principle 2 — Learn by Building

Reading alone is not enough.

Every chapter should end with building something.

Small projects help transform knowledge into practical skills.

---

## Principle 3 — Practice Before Looking at Solutions

Struggling with a problem is part of learning.

Always attempt a solution before reading the answer.

A wrong solution teaches more than copying the correct one.

---

## Principle 4 — Think Like an Engineer

Programming is not just writing code.

It's about:

- Breaking down problems.
- Choosing the right approach.
- Writing readable code.
- Optimizing when necessary.
- Explaining your decisions.

These are the skills expected in real software engineering.

---

## Principle 5 — Consistency Beats Intensity

Learning for one hour every day is more effective than studying ten hours once a week.

Small, consistent progress leads to long-term success.
